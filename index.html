Rise Up — NFT platform for artists

A marketplace where singers and artists mint their music and artwork as NFTs and sell straight to fans — with royalties paid automatically on every resale. Built the ERC-721 contracts for minting, ownership and royalty splits, wallet-based sign-in, IPFS for decentralized media, and a React + Tailwind storefront for browsing and minting.
